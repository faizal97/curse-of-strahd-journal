## Channel Divinity — which one?
- **Nature's Wrath** (action, 10 ft): restrain one enemy (STR or DEX save DC 13). Hold a dangerous one down.
- **Turn the Faithless** (action, 30 ft): **fey and fiends only, NOT undead.** Bonus: **reveals true form** if shapeshifted or under an illusion. Use on a suspected impostor.
- **Harness Divine Power** (bonus): get 1 spell slot back. Use when you're out of smites.

## Reactions
- **Sentinel:** enemy within 5 ft attacks someone else → you attack it. Your opportunity attacks drop its speed to 0, and Disengage doesn't save it.
- → **Escort role: stand next to Ireena.** Anything that swings at her eats your sword.

## Default combat turn
1. **Round 1:** Bless (action) if the party is in melee. Otherwise attack + Compelled Duel / Shield of Faith (bonus).
2. **Later rounds:** Attack. Smite **undead** (3d8) or anything about to kill someone. Save the last slot for an emergency.
3. **Ally down:** Lay on Hands (action), 5–10 HP to get them up.
4. **Out of slots:** Harness Divine Power (bonus) for one more smite.

## Opening scene plan — "Open the door" (end of S5)
You're asleep. Bromli is on watch alone. **DM rule: if Bromli wakes the others, everyone gets disadvantage.** So you may not be woken at all, and if you are, you fight at disadvantage.
1. **If woken: Divine Sense** (action). Shows undead/fiend within 60 ft **not behind total cover**. A closed door blocks it, so this checks the "Ireena" and "Ismark" **inside** the room, not what's outside.
2. **Insight +3** on them. Ask: "And Ireena — is she safe if we open it?" Test the real Ismark's habit of joking.
3. Send someone upstairs to check the real siblings are in bed.
4. **Don't open the door.** If you must: everyone up, shield on, stand by the real Ireena (Sentinel).
5. If one of them is a fake and is fey/fiend: **Turn the Faithless** reveals its true form.

## What Duff knows in character
Duff joined at S5 (the night at the Burgomaster's house). He was **not** there for S1–S3.
- **Saw himself:** the ghost child outside the study window, Kolyan's study and the hidden writ of passage, the candles flickering upstairs.
- **Only heard about (if the party told him):** the wolves, Kolyan's death, the burning of the dead, Bildrath looting, the Vistani at the tavern, Ismark's request.
- **Doesn't know:** anything the party hasn't told him. Avoid acting on player-only knowledge (metagaming).
- **Good roleplay:** ask the party to fill you in. "Who was Kolyan? Why are we guarding his daughter?" You get the info legitimately, and it's a natural scene for a newcomer.
- **Fresh eyes on Ismark and Ireena.** You don't share the party's history with them, so you're the best one to test whether the two at the door are really them.

## Roleplay hooks (from your sheet)
- **Bond: never leaves a friend behind.** You'll go back for anyone.
- **Flaw: little respect for non-warriors.** Natural friction with **Ismark** (not a fighter). Respect for **Ireena** (armoured, brave).
- **Ideal: independence; following orders blindly is tyranny.** Fits your view of **Parriwimple** (obeys Bildrath blindly) — pity, not contempt.
- **Haunted by war, crude humour.** A village full of fresh graves and pyre smoke will hit you hard, even if you weren't there for it.
- **Officer rank.** Kolyan's men were soldiers. Survivors may defer to you.
- **Oath tenets:** mercy, kindness, protect beauty and laughter, be the light. Being kind to Parriwimple and Ireena is on-oath.
- Faith: "Waves of Love". Order: Evergreen Dawn (paladins who worship greenery).
